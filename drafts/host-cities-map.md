Straight-line distances are measured between city centres. Road and flight distances are longer.

## Reading the map

- **The south-east cluster** of Rio de Janeiro, São Paulo and Belo Horizonte is the closest group, and the only one where travelling by road between cities is realistic.
- **The north-east** cities of Fortaleza, Recife and Salvador lie along the Atlantic coast, more than 1,000 km from Rio.
- **Brasília** sits on the central plateau, and **Porto Alegre** is far to the south, closer to Uruguay than to Rio.
