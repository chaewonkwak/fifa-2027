The 2027 Women's World Cup is spread across eight Brazilian cities, from Fortaleza, less than 4° south of the equator, to Porto Alegre in the far south. The map shows where each one sits, coloured by region.

Straight-line (great-circle) distances are measured between the city coordinates listed on Wikipedia, rounded to the nearest 10 km. Road and flight distances are longer.

## Reading the map

- **The south-east cluster** of Rio de Janeiro, São Paulo and Belo Horizonte is the closest group: Rio is about 360 km from São Paulo and about 340 km from Belo Horizonte, and São Paulo and Belo Horizonte are about 490 km apart.
- **The north-east** cities of Fortaleza, Recife and Salvador lie along the Atlantic coast, more than 1,000 km from Rio.
- **Brasília** is inland, in the Brazilian Highlands of the Central-West region. **Porto Alegre** is far to the south: about 350 km in a straight line from Jaguarão on the Uruguayan border, against about 1,120 km from Rio.

For travel between regions, see [getting between host cities](/travel/getting-between-cities/). Each city also has its own guide, linked in the table.
