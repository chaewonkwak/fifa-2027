Brazil's Marta is the all-time top scorer at the FIFA Women's World Cup, with 17 goals. Birgit Prinz of Germany and Abby Wambach of the United States share second place on 14 goals, and Michelle Akers of the United States is fourth with 12. The totals on this page run to the end of the most recent tournament, in 2023.

## Goals per match

Scoring rates differ more than the totals suggest. For the players whose match counts we could confirm in two independent sources:

| Player | Goals | Matches | Goals per match |
|---|---|---|---|
| Michelle Akers | 12 | 13 | 0.92 |
| Abby Wambach | 14 | 25 | 0.56 |
| Bettina Wiegmann | 11 | 22 | 0.50 |

Marta is missing from this table on purpose. Most sources give her 20 matches, but those counts were written before the 2023 tournament, where she also played without scoring. We have not found two sources that agree on her total since then.

## Other scoring records

- **Most goals at one tournament:** 10, by Michelle Akers at the first Women's World Cup in 1991.
- **Most goals in one match:** 5. Akers did it first, in 1991, and Alex Morgan of the United States matched it against Thailand in 2019.
