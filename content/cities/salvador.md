Salvador, capital of Bahia, was Brazil's first capital and is the heart of Afro-Brazilian culture. Its historic centre, Pelourinho, is a UNESCO World Heritage site above the Bay of All Saints.

## The stadium

Arena Fonte Nova stands beside the Dique do Tororó lagoon, close to the historic centre. It was rebuilt on the site of the old Fonte Nova stadium for the 2014 men's World Cup.

## Matches in Salvador

FIFA has confirmed that every host city stages at least seven matches, including at least one knockout match. Arena Fonte Nova is scheduled to host:

- Group-stage matches
- Knockout matches up to the round of 16

Teams and kickoff times are not set yet. The final draw on 11 December 2026 will decide the groups.

## Getting to the stadium

The stadium's central location helps: it is close to the historic centre, and the metro has a station within walking distance. Taxis and ride-hailing are easy from the coastal neighbourhoods.

## Arriving in Salvador

Salvador International Airport (SSA) is north-east of the centre, so allow time for the transfer to the city's hotel areas.

## Where to stay

Barra and Rio Vermelho are popular coastal bases with good restaurants, while staying in or near Pelourinho puts you close to both the old town and the stadium.

## Weather in June and July

Salvador is warm all year, and June and July fall in its rainier months. Expect warm days with regular showers, so pack light rain gear. See the [weather guide](/travel/weather-june-july/) to compare all eight host cities.
