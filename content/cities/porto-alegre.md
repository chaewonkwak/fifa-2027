Porto Alegre is the capital of Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil's southernmost state, on the shore of the Guaíba. It has strong gaúcho traditions, from barbecue to chimarrão, the local herbal tea.

## The stadium

Estádio Beira-Rio, home of Internacional, stands on the Guaíba waterfront south of the city centre. It hosted matches at the 2014 men's World Cup.

## Matches in Porto Alegre

FIFA has confirmed that every host city stages at least seven matches, including at least one knockout match. Beira-Rio is scheduled to host:

- Group-stage matches
- Knockout matches up to the round of 16

Teams and kickoff times are not set yet. The final draw on 11 December 2026 will decide the groups.

## Getting to the stadium

The stadium is a short taxi or ride-hailing trip from the centre and from neighbourhoods such as Cidade Baixa. Some fans walk along the waterfront, depending on where they stay.

## Arriving in Porto Alegre

Porto Alegre's Salgado Filho International Airport (POA) is north of the centre. It closed after severe floods in 2024 and later reopened; check current flight options when you book.

## Where to stay

Moinhos de Vento is leafy and close to restaurants, while Cidade Baixa is livelier at night and nearer the stadium.

## Weather in June and July

Porto Alegre is the coldest host city in June and July. Expect cool, sometimes cold days, chilly nights and rainy spells. Pack a proper jacket for evening matches. See the [weather guide](/travel/weather-june-july/) to compare all eight host cities.
