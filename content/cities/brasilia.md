Brasília is Brazil's planned capital, built from scratch on the central plateau and inaugurated in 1960. Its layout of wide avenues and modernist buildings is unlike any other city in the country.

## The stadium

The Estádio Nacional, also known as Mané Garrincha, sits on the city's central Monumental Axis, close to the TV Tower. It was rebuilt for the 2014 men's World Cup.

## Matches in Brasília

FIFA has confirmed that every host city stages at least seven matches, including at least one knockout match. The Estádio Nacional is scheduled to host:

- Brazil's third group match, on Sunday 4 July 2027
- Group-stage matches
- Knockout matches up to the round of 16

Teams and kickoff times are not set yet. The final draw on 11 December 2026 will decide the groups.

## Getting to the stadium

The stadium is in the middle of the planned city, so many hotels in the central hotel sectors are within walking distance or a short ride. Brasília is built around cars, so ride-hailing and taxis are common for longer trips.

## Arriving in Brasília

Brasília International Airport (BSB) is south of the centre and is one of Brazil's main domestic hubs, with frequent flights to every other host city.

## Where to stay

The North and South hotel sectors (Setor Hoteleiro Norte and Sul) are the obvious choice for a match trip, close to both the stadium and the Monumental Axis.

## Weather in June and July

June and July fall in Brasília's dry season. Expect sunny, warm days, cool nights and very low humidity. Drink plenty of water and bring lip balm and sunscreen. See the [weather guide](/travel/weather-june-july/) to compare all eight host cities.
