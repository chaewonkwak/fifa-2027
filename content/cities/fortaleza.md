Fortaleza is the capital of Ceará, on Brazil's north-east coast near the equator. It is a beach city known for its waterfront, its seafood and its warm, breezy climate.

## The stadium

Arena Castelão is south of the city centre, several kilometres inland from the beachfront. It was rebuilt for the 2014 men's World Cup and is one of the largest stadiums in the region.

## Matches in Fortaleza

FIFA has confirmed that every host city stages at least seven matches, including at least one knockout match. Arena Castelão is scheduled to host:

- Group-stage matches
- Knockout matches including a quarter-final

Teams and kickoff times are not set yet. The final draw on 11 December 2026 will decide the groups.

## Getting to the stadium

The stadium is a fair distance from the beachfront hotel areas, so most fans go by taxi, ride-hailing or bus. Check official match-day transport plans closer to the tournament.<!-- VERIFY: match-day transport options, including any light rail or shuttle -->

## Arriving in Fortaleza

Fortaleza International Airport (FOR) is south of the centre, a relatively short ride from both the hotel areas and the stadium.

## Where to stay

Meireles and Praia de Iracema, along the beachfront, are the main hotel areas, with promenades, restaurants and night markets close by.<!-- VERIFY: neighbourhood advice reviewed by a person who knows Fortaleza -->

## Weather in June and July

Fortaleza is warm all year. June and July come at the end of the rainy season, so expect hot days, a sea breeze and the chance of showers. See the [weather guide](/travel/weather-june-july/) to compare all eight host cities.
