Recife is the capital of Pernambuco on Brazil's north-east coast, a city of rivers, bridges and long urban beaches. Its historic neighbour, Olinda, is a short trip away.

## The stadium

Arena de Pernambuco is in São Lourenço da Mata, outside Recife to the west, rather than in the city itself. It was built for the 2014 men's World Cup.

## Matches in Recife

FIFA has confirmed that every host city stages at least seven matches, including at least one knockout match. Arena de Pernambuco is scheduled to host:

- Group-stage matches
- Knockout matches up to the round of 16

Teams and kickoff times are not set yet. The final draw on 11 December 2026 will decide the groups.

## Getting to the stadium

Because the stadium is outside the city, plan the trip in advance. In 2014 fans combined the metro with shuttle buses; check the official match-day transport plan for 2027 before you go.<!-- VERIFY: 2027 match-day transport plan (metro + shuttle or other) -->

## Arriving in Recife

Recife/Guararapes International Airport (REC) is close to Boa Viagem in the south of the city, which makes arrivals quick for most visitors.

## Where to stay

Boa Viagem, along the beach, is the main hotel area. The historic centre and nearby Olinda suit visitors who want more character, though they are further from the airport.<!-- VERIFY: neighbourhood advice reviewed by a person who knows Recife -->

## Weather in June and July

Recife is warm year-round, and June and July are part of its rainy season. Expect warm days and frequent showers, so pack light rain gear. See the [weather guide](/travel/weather-june-july/) to compare all eight host cities.
