Belo Horizonte is the capital of Minas Gerais, a hilly city ringed by mountains in Brazil's south-east. It is known for its bar culture, its regional cooking and as a gateway to the historic towns of the state.

## The stadium

The Mineirão is in the Pampulha district in the north of the city, near the Pampulha lake and its modernist buildings. It hosted a 2014 men's World Cup semi-final.

## Matches in Belo Horizonte

FIFA has confirmed that every host city stages at least seven matches, including at least one knockout match. The Mineirão is scheduled to host:

- Group-stage matches
- Knockout matches including a quarter-final

Teams and kickoff times are not set yet. The final draw on 11 December 2026 will decide the groups.

## Getting to the stadium

There is no metro line to the Mineirão, so most fans travel by bus, taxi or ride-hailing. Allow extra time on match days, as traffic around Pampulha builds up.<!-- VERIFY: match-day shuttle or bus plans -->

## Arriving in Belo Horizonte

Most flights use Belo Horizonte/Confins (CNF), which lies well outside the city, so budget time for the road transfer. Pampulha airport (PLU), close to the stadium, handles a small number of regional flights.<!-- VERIFY: current airlines and transfer options -->

## Where to stay

Savassi and Lourdes, south of the centre, have the best choice of hotels, bars and restaurants. Pampulha is closer to the stadium but has fewer places to stay.<!-- VERIFY: neighbourhood advice reviewed by a person who knows Belo Horizonte -->

## Weather in June and July

June and July are dry in Belo Horizonte. Days are usually sunny and mild, while nights can turn cool, so a jacket is useful for evening matches. See the [weather guide](/travel/weather-june-july/) to compare all eight host cities.
