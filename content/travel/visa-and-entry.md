Entry rules depend on your passport, and they can change. Treat this page as a starting point and confirm on Brazil's official government pages before you travel. This is general information, not legal advice.

## US, Canadian and Australian citizens

Since 10 April 2025, citizens of the United States, Canada and Australia need a visa to visit Brazil for tourism or business. They can apply online for an electronic visitor visa (e-Visa) through the official portal, [brazil.vfsevisa.com](https://brazil.vfsevisa.com/).

According to Brazil's consular pages, the application asks for:

- A passport valid until the end of your trip, with two free visa pages
- The online application form
- A passport-style photo
- The e-Visa fee of US$80.90

The e-Visa covers stays of up to 90 days. Apply well before your trip, since processing takes a few days.

## Everyone else

Many nationalities can visit Brazil without a visa for short stays, but not all. Check the rules for your passport on the website of the Brazilian embassy or consulate in your country, under the Ministry of Foreign Affairs at gov.br.

## On arrival

Carry your passport, proof of onward travel and your accommodation details. Your match tickets are not a visa.
