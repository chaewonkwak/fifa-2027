Brazil is enormous, and the eight host cities are spread across it, from Porto Alegre in the far south to Fortaleza in the north-east. Most fans following a team will need to fly.

## Flying

Domestic flights are the practical way to cover most distances. Brasília and São Paulo are major hubs with connections to every other host city. Book early: demand around match days will be high.

## By road

Long-distance buses are comfortable and common in Brazil, and they work well on shorter routes. Rio de Janeiro and São Paulo are the most practical pair by road. Most other combinations are long overnight journeys.

## The three regions

It helps to think of the host cities in three groups:

- **South-east**: Rio de Janeiro, São Paulo and Belo Horizonte, the closest cluster.
- **North-east**: Fortaleza, Recife and Salvador, along the Atlantic coast.
- **Centre and south**: Brasília on the central plateau and Porto Alegre in the far south.

Moving within a group is easier than between groups. Once the draw on 11 December 2026 sets the groups, check where your team plays before booking.

## Following Brazil

Brazil's group matches are in Rio de Janeiro (24 June), São Paulo (29 June) and Brasília (4 July). That is one short hop between Rio and São Paulo, then a flight to the capital.
