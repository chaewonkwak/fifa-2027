The tournament falls in the southern hemisphere's winter. In Brazil that means very different conditions from city to city, so pack for the cities you will visit, not for "Brazil".

## City by city

| City | June–July in brief | Pack |
|---|---|---|
| Porto Alegre | The coldest host city: cool to cold days, chilly nights, rainy spells | A warm jacket |
| São Paulo | Mild days, chilly nights, grey drizzly spells | A warm layer |
| Belo Horizonte | Dry, sunny, mild days and cool nights | A light jacket |
| Brasília | Dry season: sunny days, cool nights, very low humidity | Sunscreen, water, lip balm |
| Rio de Janeiro | Mild and often sunny, cooler evenings | A light layer |
| Salvador | Warm, with regular showers | Light rain gear |
| Recife | Warm, rainy season | Light rain gear |
| Fortaleza | Hot with a sea breeze, end of the rainy season | Sun protection, light rain gear |

## Night matches

Evening kickoffs in the south and south-east can feel cold, especially in Porto Alegre and São Paulo. In the north-east, rain is the bigger concern.
