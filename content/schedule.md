FIFA published the match schedule on 20 August 2026. It fixes the dates and venues of all 64 matches, but not the teams: those are set by the final draw on 11 December 2026.

## Tournament dates

The tournament runs from Thursday 24 June to Sunday 25 July 2027. It starts with eight groups of four teams. The top two in each group reach the round of 16, followed by quarter-finals, semi-finals, a third-place match and the final.

## Venues by round

| Round | Host cities |
|---|---|
| Opening match | Rio de Janeiro (Maracanã) |
| Group stage | All eight host cities |
| Round of 16 | Including Brasília, Porto Alegre, Recife and Salvador, whose last matches are in this round |
| Quarter-finals | Belo Horizonte, Fortaleza, Rio de Janeiro, São Paulo |
| Semi-finals | Rio de Janeiro and São Paulo |
| Third-place match | São Paulo |
| Final | Rio de Janeiro (Maracanã), 25 July 2027 |

Every host city stages at least seven matches, including at least one knockout match.


## Brazil's group matches

| Date | Stadium |
|---|---|
| Thursday 24 June 2027 | Estádio do Maracanã, Rio de Janeiro |
| Tuesday 29 June 2027 | Arena Itaquera, São Paulo |
| Sunday 4 July 2027 | Estádio Nacional, Brasília |

Brazil's opponents will be known after the final draw.

## Kickoff times

Kickoff times have not been announced yet. All eight host cities use Brasília time (UTC-3). The [kickoff times page](/kickoff-times/) converts Brasília time to your time zone.
