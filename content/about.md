The Brazil 2027 Fan Guide is an independent, English-language guide for fans planning to follow the 2027 Women's World Cup in Brazil, in person or from home.

## Who we are

<!-- VERIFY: write a short, true description of the person or people behind the site, using the pen name set in site.json -->
The guide is run by a small independent team of football fans. We are not affiliated with FIFA, the local organisers, any team or any ticket seller.

## How we write and check

- Every fact about the tournament links to its source, usually an official FIFA or Brazilian government page.
- Each source shows the date we last checked it, and pages show when they were last updated.
- We use AI tools to help draft and organise pages. A person reviews and edits every page before it is published.
- We do not invent figures. Where something is not announced yet, we say so.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell tickets, and we do not link to ticket resellers.
- We do not run ads or paid placements.

## Corrections

If something is wrong or out of date, please tell us through the [contact page](/contact/). Corrections are listed on the [sources page](/sources/).
