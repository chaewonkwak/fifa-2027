FIFA organises many tournaments, and people search for them in many ways. In 2027 the biggest one by far is the FIFA Women's World Cup, so that is what most searches for "FIFA 2027" are about.

## The 2027 Women's World Cup

The tenth FIFA Women's World Cup will be played in Brazil from 24 June to 25 July 2027. It is the first edition in South America. Thirty-two national teams from all six confederations will play 64 matches in eight host cities, with the opening match and the final at the Maracanã in Rio de Janeiro. Spain are the defending champions. Brazil's Marta is the tournament's [all-time top scorer](/womens-world-cup-top-scorers/).

The rest of this site covers the tournament in detail: the [schedule](/schedule/), the [host cities](/#cities), [tickets](/tickets/) and [travel](/travel/visa-and-entry/).

## Looking for the video game?

There is no new game called "FIFA 27". EA's football series has been called EA SPORTS FC since 2023, and its 2026 release is EA SPORTS FC 27, which came out on 25 September 2026. FIFA now licenses its name to other games instead; one example is FIFA Rivals, a mobile game released in 2025.

## Looking for the men's World Cup?

The men's World Cup was held in Canada, Mexico and the United States in June and July 2026. The next men's edition is in 2030.
