Thirty-two teams will play in Brazil. Hosts Brazil qualified automatically, and the other places are decided through each confederation's qualifying competition and an inter-confederation play-off.

## Qualified so far

## How the remaining places are decided

- **Concacaf** (North and Central America and the Caribbean): the Concacaf W Championship, from late November to early December 2026.
- **UEFA**: play-off rounds in late 2026.
- **Inter-confederation play-off**: six teams compete in February 2027 for the last three places.

## The draw

The final draw on 11 December 2026 sorts the 32 teams into eight groups of four. Some places will still be filled by play-off winners at that point.
