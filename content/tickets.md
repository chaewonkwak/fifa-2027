Tickets are sold only through FIFA's official ticketing site, [FIFA.com/tickets](https://www.fifa.com/tickets). This page summarises what FIFA has announced; always check the official site before you buy. We do not sell tickets or link to resellers.

## Sales phases

| Phase | Dates | What is on sale |
|---|---|---|
| Opening phase | 1 to 8 December 2026, from 10:00 Brasília time | Ticket packages, first come, first served |
| Visa presale | After the final draw | For Visa cardholders |
| Two further phases | To be announced | Remaining tickets for all fans |

## The three packages

- **Brazil Team Pass**: tickets to all three of Brazil's group matches. From BRL 205 (about USD 36) in category 3.
- **Arena Pass**: matches at one stadium, up to and including its quarter-final if it has one. FIFA says it saves 27% compared with single tickets.
- **Flex Pass**: three eligible matches of your choice. It can include one Brazil group match or one semi-final, but not the final or the third-place match.

Tickets inside packages start at BRL 30 (about USD 5) per match in category 3, depending on the matches chosen.

## Good to know

- Prices are in Brazilian reais and include tax.
- Visa is FIFA's preferred payment method; other methods are also accepted in the opening phase.
- Half-price tickets are available to groups eligible under Brazilian law.
- Accessibility tickets, including wheelchair and extra-width seats, are on sale from the start.
- FIFA warns fans to avoid unauthorised websites, social media sellers and other intermediaries.
