Brazil's Marta is the all-time top scorer at the FIFA Women's World Cup, with 17 goals in 23 matches across six tournaments. Birgit Prinz of Germany and Abby Wambach of the United States share second place on 14 goals, and Michelle Akers of the United States is fourth with 12. The totals on this page run to the end of the most recent tournament, in 2023.

## Goals per match

Scoring rates differ more than the totals suggest. For the players whose match counts we could confirm:

| Player | Goals | Matches | Goals per match |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marta | 17 | 23 | 0.74 |
| Abby Wambach | 14 | 25 | 0.56 |
| Bettina Wiegmann | 11 | 22 | 0.50 |

Marta's 23 matches include the 2023 tournament, her sixth. Many older lists give her 20 matches, which was her total before 2023.

## Other scoring records

- **Most goals at one tournament:** 10, by Michelle Akers at the first Women's World Cup in 1991.
- **Most goals in one match:** 5. Akers did it first, in 1991, and Alex Morgan of the United States matched it against Thailand in 2019.
