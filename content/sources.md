We build every page from official announcements wherever possible, and link the source next to the facts it supports.

## Main sources

- **FIFA** ([inside.fifa.com](https://inside.fifa.com)): match schedule, host cities, tickets, draw dates.
- **FIFA ticketing** ([FIFA.com/tickets](https://www.fifa.com/tickets)): the only official place to buy tickets.
- **Brazil's Ministry of Foreign Affairs** (consulate pages on gov.br): visa and entry rules.

When an official source and a news report disagree, we follow the official source and say so.
