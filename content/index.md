The 2027 FIFA Women's World Cup runs from **24 June to 25 July 2027** in Brazil, the first edition held in South America. Thirty-two teams will play 64 matches across eight host cities. The opening match and the final are both at Rio de Janeiro's Maracanã.

This is an independent fan guide. It collects the practical information visitors need, with a link to the official source behind every fact.

## Key dates

| What | When |
|---|---|
| First ticket sales phase | 1 to 8 December 2026 |
| Final draw (groups decided) | 11 December 2026 |
| Opening match, Brazil at the Maracanã | 24 June 2027 |
| Final at the Maracanã | 25 July 2027 |

More detail is in the [schedule](/schedule/) and [tickets](/tickets/) guides.
