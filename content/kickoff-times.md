FIFA has not announced kickoff times yet. When it does, they will be in local Brazilian time. All eight host cities, from Porto Alegre in the south to Fortaleza in the north-east, use Brasília time, which is UTC-3. Brazil does not use daylight saving time.

## Quick reference: a 16:00 kickoff in Brasília

During the tournament it is summer in the northern hemisphere, so the US, UK and Europe are on daylight saving time.

| Where | Time zone in June–July | 16:00 Brasília is |
|---|---|---|
| Los Angeles | PDT (UTC-7) | 12:00 |
| New York | EDT (UTC-4) | 15:00 |
| London | BST (UTC+1) | 20:00 |
| Paris, Berlin, Madrid | CEST (UTC+2) | 21:00 |
| Seoul, Tokyo | KST / JST (UTC+9) | 04:00 next day |
| Sydney | AEST (UTC+10) | 05:00 next day |

To use the table for another kickoff time, shift every row by the same number of hours.
